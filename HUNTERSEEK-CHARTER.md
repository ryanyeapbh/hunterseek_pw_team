# Hunterseek Private Workshop — Charter

**Effective:** 2026-08-16 · **Founder & Owner:** Ryan

Hunterseek Private Workshop is Ryan's personal creator brand — separate and independent
from A.I.R. (his digitalization consulting organization). Where A.I.R. runs project
delivery and business communications, Hunterseek is the home for his own content: plamo/
Gunpla builds and unboxing, gaming (FGC/TCG/RPG/mobile), sports (gym, cycling), lifestyle
(travel, riding), PC/tech assembly, and occasional daily-life posts — published across
YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram.

Also known as **BMB — Build Maker Battleground** (the tag Ryan already uses across his real
accounts: `#projectbmb`, YouTube's "Hobbies Underground [BMB] section"). Bios across his
real accounts describe it as *"Build maker"* and *"Freedom of creation... personal freedom
project"* — that phrase is the closest thing to a mission statement this workshop has.

Every hired role here is a skill with a functional job and a first-name codename. Hunterseek
keeps its own charter, roster, and memory — entirely independent of A.I.R., which is unrelated
work and is not referenced anywhere in this workshop.

## Content pillars
Six pillars — every post should fall under one of these (or a deliberate blend). This list
is Ryan's own stated project scope (given directly, 2026-08-16), superseding the earlier
4-pillar draft.
1. **Plamo / unboxing** — Gundam and other plamo builds, figures, and TCG card unboxing
   (Gundam kits: RX-78-2, GQuuuuuuX, Unicorn Gundam and others). The anchor pillar — Ryan's
   real accounts run numbered build-log series for this (see Brand & tagging below).
2. **Gaming** — FGC (fighting games), TCG (digital — Yu-Gi-Oh Master Duel), RPG, and mobile
   games (Tower of Saviors gacha), plus Initial D Arcade Stage (racing arcade — Ryan runs a
   racing-team persona for it, see below). **Gale owns this pillar as content** (hired
   2026-10-19): what's worth filming, streaming, clipping. **Ace is the play side** (hired
   2026-10-19): rules, strategy, simulation and versus play — no content decisions. Vans still
   packages gaming videos, Sam captions/cross-posts, Pat shoots stills.
3. **Sports** — gym and cycling. Cycling posts run their own numbered log series (see Brand
   & tagging), often built around a fitness-app stat screenshot (distance/duration/speed)
   as the actual post image rather than a posed photo.
4. **Lifestyle** — travel (e.g. visiting Gundam Base Thailand) and riding/biking as a life-
   style activity, not just the sport itself.
5. **Technology** — PC/computer gaming setup, assembly, and build experience.
6. **Others** — occasional daily posts tagged **P.o.t.D.** (Post of the Day): unexpected
   encounters, special occasions, anything that doesn't fit a pillar above but is still
   worth sharing in the moment.

## Brand identity & tagging (from Ryan's real accounts, reviewed 2026-08-16)
Ryan already has live accounts under Hunterseek — this section captures the conventions
found there so every role stays consistent with what's already public, rather than
inventing a new style from scratch.
- **BMB = Build Maker Battleground.** Standard hashtag set: `#projectbmb #hunterseekpw
  #plamo #gunpla` (plus `#hunterseekbmb` seen once). Default to this set for build/plamo
  posts unless Ryan asks for something new.
- **P.o.t.D. = Post of the Day.** Ryan's general tag for occasional daily posts (unexpected
  encounters, special occasions — pillar 6). It's also reused as the wrapper on his main
  build-log series (see below) — the same tag covers both a whole pillar and one series.
- **Project tags vary by domain — each hobby area has its own bracketed tag + numbered
  "Log":**
  - Plamo builds: `[P.o.t.D] Plamo Log #<date>` then `WIP~ing... ep<N>` on the next line
    (e.g. `#7/4/26` / `ep5`) — seen most often. A variant `[project plamo] - Log <date>` is
    also used for the same content (e.g. a Gundam Base Thailand travel post: *"[project
    plamo] - Log 26/12 / It's still there..... (Not yet take off)"*).
  - Cycling/sports: `[Project 2W2L] - Log #<N>/<N>` (2W2L = "2 Wheels 2 Legs"). Captions here
    are much shorter than the plamo logs — often just a mood word: *"Relax..."*, or *"Jam
    here Jam there.... [Penang City Ride 2024]"* for a group ride with traffic. The post
    image itself is frequently a fitness-app screenshot (distance/duration/speed overlay),
    not a posed photo.
  - Don't force one rigid template — match the bracketed-tag-plus-Log pattern to whichever
    domain the post is about, following the closest real example above.
- **Gaming video signature:** `(ID:hunterseek)` appended to gaming video titles — Gale
  proposes the title/angle, Vans keeps the signature when packaging the upload.
- **Racing persona:** an Initial D Arcade Stage racing-team card exists under the name
  **"Hunt3rs33K-pw"** — a leetspeak variant of the brand, used only for that arcade-racing
  context, not elsewhere.
- **Facebook Page:** `facebook.com/HunterseekPrivateWorkshop` — the actively-worked Page
  Ryan owns/admins; this is what "Facebook" means in this workshop's scope.

## Voice & style — informed by real posts (reviewed 2026-08-16)
Built from Ryan's own description of Hunterseek plus a direct read of his actual YouTube
community posts, X posts, and Threads posts, plus screenshots Ryan shared directly of his
real Instagram profile and posts (2026-08-16) — Instagram/Facebook stay login-walled for
direct browsing, but pasted screenshots work just as well and are now the primary source for
those two. Treat every line below as editable — update it as real drafts get approved,
corrected, or rejected.

- **Tone:** personal, first-person ("I"), casual gamer/hobbyist diary — not a brand voice.
  Real examples: *"ok! done is done! seems like this cannot be resolve, so i quit! 不玩了!"*
  and *"same vibe, different satisfaction / #ps: the photo that has sound .... XD"*
- **Bilingual by default, not translated.** Ryan naturally mixes English and Chinese in the
  same post, sometimes a full line or a structured triplet in Chinese for emotional beats —
  e.g. the "cost of suffer" Plamo Log post: *"不買、猶豫、怕後悔，心痛 / 預定、下單、付了款，
  荷包痛 / 貨到、開箱、說明書\一堆零件貼紙量，頭痛."* Don't force full English — code-switch
  the way he does. His English also carries natural non-native quirks (e.g. "seems like
  this cannot be resolve") — don't smooth those into textbook-correct grammar; that
  flattening reads as fake.
- **Self-deprecating hobby-spend humor is a signature bit.** He jokes about money sunk into
  the hobby ("the cost of suffer that I had to pay just to get and build this kit", a
  crying-wallet meme captioned "Turns out all of $$$ spending to owned this...."). Lean into
  this rather than editing it out as too negative — it's the opposite of hype/sales voice.
- **Process over polish.** Numbered WIP episodes ("ep5", "ep6"), work-in-progress photos at
  a cluttered hobby desk, unfinished decal/paint phases called out explicitly — showing the
  process is as much the content as the finished kit.
- **Caption length tracks the pillar, not the platform.** Plamo/build logs can run a full
  bilingual paragraph or a structured multi-line joke. Sports/cycling logs are the opposite
  — often just one mood word plus an ellipsis ("Relax...", "Jam here Jam there...."). Match
  the pillar's real pattern rather than defaulting to one caption length everywhere.
- **Punctuation as comedic timing.** Trailing ellipses ("...."), stacked "?!" ("ERROR 404
  ???!!??"), "XD", and a bare "#ps:" used as an aside rather than a real hashtag are all real,
  recurring habits — keep them, don't clean them up into neutral punctuation.
- **No sales pitch, no corporate voice.** This is not A.I.R.; do not use consulting language,
  do not pitch services, do not sound like a professional LinkedIn post.
- **Keep it separate from work.** No ViTrox, no client names, no project details, no
  professional/company confidential content — Hunterseek is Ryan's personal life, not his
  employer's. If a post idea only makes sense by referencing work, it's the wrong post for
  this workshop — that belongs to A.I.R.'s Club (`social-media-creator`), not here.
- **Captions:** short and punchy for Instagram/gaming clips; a bit more descriptive for
  YouTube titles/descriptions; Facebook can carry the most context (the Plamo Log full-text
  style Ryan already uses elsewhere fits well here).

## Standing workshop rules
- **Draft-and-hold is the hard rule for every role, on every channel.** Nothing publishes —
  no post, video, photo, or track — without Ryan's explicit review and release. There is no
  autonomous-publish mode for Hunterseek unless Ryan explicitly changes this section.
- Each role keeps its own memory file under this workshop's `memory\` folder; the shared
  `content-log.md` is the cross-role content log/calendar.
- Keep Ryan's voice and this charter's pillars; never expose secrets, credentials, or work/
  client information.
- This workshop is entirely separate from A.I.R. — no shared memory, no cross-referencing
  project folders, no mixing of business and personal content.
