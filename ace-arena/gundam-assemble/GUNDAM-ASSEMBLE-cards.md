## What the abilities actually change

**Free extra attacks are the real currency.** Three cards/abilities hand you attacks at **TL 0**:

- **T10 Crimson Execution** + **Char's Zaku II**: Dash (TL 0) → Heat Hawk (TL 0). A dash-then-hit with no timeline cost. Best tempo card in the set, and it's a Command card — so it stacks on top of his normal Primary Action.
- **T03 Iron-Blooded Tenacity** + **Barbatos**: survive at 1 HP, then Move 2 and swing Rex Claws at TL 0. A free "I don't die" plus a free attack. Save it for when he gets focused.
- **Barbatos Annihilate**: after a Tail Blade attack, another Rex Claws at TL 0. Tail Blade (4 TL) + Claws (0) = two attacks for the price of one, though the second is the weak 3-Strength claw.

**Vidar's Handguns are a machine gun.** Critical → repeat the whole attack at TL 0. With 3 dice at 20% crit each, that's ~49% to re-roll the attack for free. Cheap unit, cheap turns (TL 1), reloads itself.

**Wing Zero's crit engine is the best in the box.** Zero System makes 7s and 8s crit too → 40% per die. Twin Buster (Str 6) averages ~2.4 crits → **Damage +2.4** on average, and the crit damage is +1 each, max +4. Expected output is ~6.6 damage in one 4-TL swing. Kill it before it fires twice.

**Epyon and Barbatos both Pull, Epyon Disarms.** Pull 1 sets up collisions or drags a unit out of cover; Disarm forces a re-roll of all hits next attack — brutal against a big single swing like Barbatos' Tail Blade.

**Status-effect synergy:** Epyon's Exploit Weakness is **+2 Strength vs. units with status effects**, and Epyon applies Slow/Disarm itself via Heat Rod. It's the one unit with a built-in combo.

**Barbatos gets scarier as it gets hurt** — Fight to the End: +1 Str at 6 damage, +2 total at 12. Chipping it to 5 HP and leaving it is the worst option: it's now a 9-Strength Tail Blade.
