## Reusable patterns / templates that worked
- Build-log format: `[P.o.t.D] Plamo Log #<date>` + `WIP~ing... ep<N>` — his own recurring
  structure (found on X/Threads), safe to reuse as a template for Facebook/Instagram too.
- Meme-repost format: short line ("same vibe, different satisfaction") + `#ps:` aside +
  reaction-meme image — used across YouTube community posts and X.
- Sports/cycling log format: `[Project 2W2L] - Log #<N>` + a terse mood-word caption
  ("Relax...", "Jam here Jam there....") — much shorter than plamo logs; sometimes the post
  image is a fitness-app stat screenshot rather than a photo.
- Travel/lifestyle: `[project plamo] - Log <date>` variant used for a Gundam Base Thailand
  visit ("It's still there..... (Not yet take off)") — shows travel content can carry a
  plamo-adjacent tag when the subject overlaps (visiting a Gundam landmark).

## Recurring pitfalls to avoid

- Don't drop the Chinese half of a bilingual post to make it "cleaner."
- Don't skip the self-deprecating hobby-spend humor — it's a signature, not a flaw to fix.
